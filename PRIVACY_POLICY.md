# Privacy Policy - Surf Social Media

**Last updated:** April 22, 2026

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Account Information
- Email address, display name, profile photo
- Authentication data (via Firebase Authentication)
- Facebook/Google OAuth tokens (if using social login)

### 1.2 User-Generated Content
- Posts, comments, messages, photos, videos
- Reactions and interactions

### 1.3 Usage Data
- IP address, device information, browser type
- Log data and analytics

## 2. How We Use Your Information

- To provide and improve our services
- To authenticate users and secure accounts
- To deliver notifications and real-time updates
- To moderate content and ensure community safety

## 3. Data Storage

- Data is stored in Firebase (Google Cloud Platform)
- Media files stored in Firebase Storage
- Real-time data via Socket.io and LiveKit

## 4. Third-Party Services

We use:
- **Firebase** (Authentication, Firestore, Storage)
- **Facebook Login** (OAuth authentication)
- **Google Sign-In** (OAuth authentication)
- **Cloudinary** (Image/video processing)
- **LiveKit** (Video calling)

## 5. Your Rights

- Access, update, or delete your account
- Export your data
- Opt-out of notifications

## 6. Contact

For privacy concerns, contact: privacy@surfsocial.media
